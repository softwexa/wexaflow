# Apply for a WexaFlow Free license

**A Free license requires an application and a product-specific tenant grant from Softwexa.**

## Submit your application

[Open the WexaFlow Free license application](https://www.softwexa.com/contact/?product=wexaflow&service=WexaFlow%20Free%20license%20application)

The website form selects **WexaFlow** and fills the subject **"WexaFlow Free license application"**. Add your name, company, work email and the details below, then submit the enquiry.

You can also send your application to [hello@softwexa.com](mailto:hello@softwexa.com?subject=WexaFlow%20Free%20license%20application).

## What to include

- Company or team name and a contact person.
- Work email.
- Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance application and platform versions, if known.
- Intended environment: development, test, sandbox or a proposed production use case.
- A short description of the business workflow you want to evaluate.
- Any additional capabilities you need to discuss.

Tenant registration and product matching are confirmed during review. Share any requested tenant details through the private application channel.

## What happens after applying?

Softwexa reviews the requested product, environment and use case. The response confirms the available Free scope, required tenant registration, package compatibility and next steps. Product access is activated through the confirmed product-specific grant.

Submitting a form or requesting a demo does not automatically approve an application, issue a key or activate a trial. The scope and terms of the product license are confirmed before activation.

## Keep application details private

Use the website form or business email for your application. Public GitHub issues are for general product questions and anonymized reports. Leave out credentials, license keys, connection strings and sensitive customer data.
